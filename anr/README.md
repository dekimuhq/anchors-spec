# Anchored Notice Receipts (ANR)

Wire type: `ar.notice.v1`

Records privacy notice delivery and acknowledgement. Six event types covering notice presentation, re-presentation, acknowledgement, withdrawal, translation, and joint-controller essence. Designed for GDPR Arts. 12-14, Art. 26, and AI Act Art. 50.

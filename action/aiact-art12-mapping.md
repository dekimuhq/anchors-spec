# AActR ↔ EU AI Act Art. 12 / Art. 26 / Art. 19 — Field Mapping (v1)

> [`action/v1.md`](v1.md) — read that first for the AActR wire format this
> document maps against.

## 0. What this document is

A **field-level correspondence table** between the EU AI Act's logging
obligations (Articles 12, 19, 26) and the fields already defined by the
Anchored Receipts envelope and the `ar.action.v1` (AActR) receipt family,
its `aiact.art12` profile, and three neighbouring families it composes
with (ACR, ADR, ARR).

It is **not** a conformity assessment, **not legal advice**, and **not** a
statement that any implementation conforms to Art. 12. See §5.

## 1. What Articles 12 / 19 / 26 ask for (summary, not a legal opinion)

| Clause | Asks for |
|---|---|
| Art. 12(1) | High-risk AI systems technically allow **automatic recording of events (logs)** over the system's lifetime. |
| Art. 12(2)(a) | Logging enables identifying situations where the system may present a risk, or a **substantial modification**. |
| Art. 12(2)(b)/(c) | Logging supports post-market monitoring and operational monitoring. |
| Art. 12(3)(a) | (Annex III(1)(a) systems) start/end date-time of **each use period**. |
| Art. 12(3)(d) | Identification of the **natural person(s) involved in verifying results** (the Art. 14(5) human overseer). |
| Art. 14 | Human oversight — a natural person can monitor, intervene, and override. |
| Art. 19 | Automatically generated logs **retained ≥ 6 months** (subject to other applicable law), available on request. |
| Art. 26(6) | Deployers retain logs generated by the system, likewise for **≥ 6 months** unless other law requires longer. |

## 2. Mapping table

| Requirement | Anchors mechanism | Field(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12(1) automatic recording of events | AActR minted per gated agent action, at the point of execution — not a manual after-the-fact entry | `ar.action.v1` envelope, minted at the door | The receipt records that an event happened; it does not itself cause the system to log — that is an implementation property outside the wire format. |
| 12(1) tamper-evidence | Envelope signature + daily Merkle anchoring | `sig` (Ed25519 / `ml-dsa-65` per envelope version), `anchor` (RFC 6962 inclusion against a signed daily root) | Altering any signed field breaks verification; altering history after anchoring breaks the log's own consistency proof. Tamper-evident by construction — it does not by itself prove no event was ever *omitted* by a dishonest issuer (§5). |
| 12(1) "not ad-hoc text" | Public, versioned wire format | `claim_type: "ar.action.v1"`, this spec | A frozen, publicly specified shape rather than free-text log lines. Whether it meets any given regulator's "recognized standard" bar is outside this document's authority to assert (§5). |
| 12(2)(a) risk-relevant events / substantial modification | `aiact.art12` profile overlay (§4) | `body.aiact_art12.risk_event` (`none` \| `risk_flagged` \| `substantial_modification`), `body.aiact_art12.system_version` | A version bump alongside a `substantial_modification` event is the intended substantial-modification signal. |
| 12(2)(b)/(c) monitoring feed | Same receipt set, queryable by `system_id` | `body.aiact_art12.system_id` | The wire format defines the field; assembling a monitoring feed from an issuer's receipt store is an application concern, not a new claim type. |
| 12(3)(a) start/end of each use | `aiact.art12` profile overlay | `body.aiact_art12.period_start` (RFC 3339, required), `body.aiact_art12.period_end` (RFC 3339, optional — set when the use period has a defined end) | For a single dispatched action, `executed_at` (§3) already carries the moment of execution; `period_start`/`period_end` exist for the profile's broader "use period" framing when a run spans more than one instant. |
| 12(3)(d) identification of the human overseer | `aiact.art12.oversight_ref` → an ACR receipt under the `aiact.art14` profile | `body.aiact_art12.oversight_ref` (`sha256:<hex>`, points at the referenced receipt's hash), ACR body fields `oversight_action` (`approved` \| `rejected` \| `escalated` \| `overridden`) and `reviewer_role_commit` (salted, non-PII role identifier) | The overseer's role is committed, not the person's raw identity in plaintext — consistent with the commit discipline the whole family uses for anything that could carry PII. |
| Art. 14 human oversight, generally | ACR (`ar.consent.v1`) under the `aiact.art14` profile; ADR (`ar.delegation.v1`) `human_oversight_uri` constraint | ACR `lawful_basis` structured triple; ADR `scope.constraints.human_oversight_uri` | Two different angles on the same obligation: ACR records a specific oversight *decision*; ADR records that a delegated agent's scope of authority *carries* a standing oversight channel. |
| Art. 19 / 26(6) ≥ 6-month log retention | ARR (Anchored Retention Receipts) family — `ar.hold.v1`, `ar.archive.v1`, `ar.expiry.v1` | ARR wire types (spec: placeholder, reference implementation shipped — see [`arr/README.md`](../arr/README.md)) | ARR is the family designed for retention-lifecycle events generally (GDPR Art. 5(1)(e)/17/12(3)); using `ar.hold.v1` to express a retention floor on an AActR/`aiact.art12` receipt set is a composition pattern, not a new claim type. The full ARR wire spec is not yet published; treat this row as directional until it is. |
| Art. 26 deployer obligations (agent descriptor, oversight, incident reporting) | ADR (`ar.delegation.v1`) under the `aiact.art26.deployer` profile | `body.agent.agent_descriptor` (`model`, `model_version`, `tool_set_hash`), `body.scope.constraints.human_oversight_uri`, `body.scope.constraints.incident_reporting_uri` | Grant-event fields on the delegation that authorized the agent to act — a broader deployer-obligation surface than the per-action log entry. |
| Per-run execution detail (supplementary, not required by Art. 12 itself) | AgTR (`ar.trace.v1`) | `body.stages[]` (named pipeline stages, duration, status), `body.request_id_commit` (joins the trace to the same run's AActR) | AgTR records execution *provenance* (what ran, in what order) for a whole run — useful supplementary detail alongside a single action's Art-12 log entry, not itself an Art-12 field. |

## 3. AActR body fields (baseline, every profile)

Every `ar.action.v1` receipt — regardless of profile — carries the fields
below (full definitions: [`action/v1.md`](v1.md) §3):

| Field | Plaintext or committed | Relevant to |
|---|---|---|
| `verb` | plaintext | 12(1) — the dispatched action is legible, not opaque |
| `outcome` (`executed` \| `failed-gave-up`) | plaintext | 12(1)/12(2)(a) — a stopped/failed action is as log-worthy as a completed one |
| `executed_at` | plaintext, RFC 3339 | 12(3)(a) — the moment of execution |
| `decision_commit` / `params_commit` / `request_id_commit` | salted `sha256` commit | 12(3)(c) — binds the exact input/target without exposing it in the anchored record |
| `credential_id` (optional) | plaintext | Links the action to the authorizing mandate; relevant to Art. 26 authority-scoping generally |
| `caveats_consumed` (optional) | plaintext bounds, non-PII | The scope of authority the action exercised |
| `agent_id` (optional) | plaintext, non-PII principal | Answers "which agent", stable across that agent's mandates |

## 4. The `aiact.art12` profile

The action family defines an additive `aiact.art12` profile: the **same**
receipt shape as the baseline `hub.automation` profile, declared as a
high-risk-AI-system log entry and carrying one additional overlay object,
`aiact_art12`:

```jsonc
{
  "profile": "aiact.art12",
  "verb": "<ns>.<op>",           // unchanged from the baseline shape
  // ...outcome / executed_at / commits, unchanged...

  "aiact_art12": {
    "system_id": "string",             // stable id of the high-risk AI system
    "system_version": "string",        // release; a change alongside risk_event: substantial_modification is the signal
    "period_start": "rfc3339",         // 12(3)(a)
    "period_end": "rfc3339",           // optional, 12(3)(a)
    "risk_event": "none | risk_flagged | substantial_modification",   // optional, 12(2)(a)
    "oversight_ref": "sha256:<hex>"    // optional, 12(3)(d) — points at an aiact.art14 receipt
  }
}
```

All `aiact_art12` fields beyond `system_id`/`system_version`/`period_start`
are optional; a validator checks internal well-formedness (non-empty
strings, RFC 3339 timestamps with `period_end >= period_start`, the
`risk_event` enum, the `oversight_ref` hash shape) — the same "well-formed,
not independently verified" posture as every other issuer-asserted field
in this family (§5).

This profile introduces no new envelope field and no new cryptographic
primitive; it is a body-level overlay on the same signed, anchored
`ar.action.v1` shape described in [`action/v1.md`](v1.md).

## 5. What this mapping is NOT

This document is a **field-level correspondence**, not a conformity
assessment and not legal advice. Reading this table as "receipt X = Art. 12
compliance" would be a misreading. Specifically:

- It does **not** state or imply that any implementation **conforms to**
  Art. 12, and it is not a certification of any kind.
- It does **not** claim this is a **recognized standard** under Art. 12(1)'s
  "not ad-hoc text files" language — that determination belongs to
  regulators and standards bodies, not to this repository.
- It does **not** claim the underlying receipts are **unforgeable**. They
  are **tamper-evident by construction** — altering a signed field or a
  chain link breaks verification — which is a different and narrower
  property than "no party, including the issuer, can ever produce a
  misleading record."
- A receipt set proves that **what was minted** is internally consistent
  and anchored; it cannot prove that an issuer minted a receipt for
  **every** action it actually took. Absence of a never-minted event is not
  something any receipt format can detect. This is precisely why Art. 12 is
  a system-level provider obligation, not a property a receipt format
  confers on its own.
- Article 12 record-keeping, Article 9 risk management, and Article 10
  data governance are three distinct obligations. This document addresses
  only the first.
- This mapping and its underlying composition are pending external
  security review. Treat every row above as **descriptive** ("records",
  "maps to", "can support"), never as a compliance guarantee.

**This is not legal advice.** Consult qualified counsel for any
determination of regulatory conformity.

## 6. Related work (honest framing, no priority claim)

Anchored, signed receipts mapped to AI-Act log fields are not a novel
category — `draft-marques-asqav-compliance-receipts` (IETF) proposes a
similar compliance-receipt-to-AI-Act-field mapping. This document is the
`ar.*`-family equivalent of that mapping, not a claim of being first or
the only such effort.

## 7. Versioning

This mapping tracks `ar.action.v1` and its `aiact.art12` profile. New rows
are additive as neighbouring families (ACR, ADR, ARR) publish their own
full specs; a breaking change to any referenced field requires updating
this document alongside the owning family's own versioning rule.
